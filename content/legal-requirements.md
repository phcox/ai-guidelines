## Complete all necessary internal reviews

> **Red Hat internal resources**
>
> Some links on this page are for Red Hat employees only. Connect to the Red Hat VPN and sign in with your Red Hat account to open them.

Red Hat teams developing AI-enabled features must follow any applicable internal review requirements. These may include an AI Assessment (AIA), a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA), or reviews by other teams. Following this public design guidance does not replace those reviews.

An AIA is Red Hat's internal review of a new or changed AI use case. For details on when an assessment is required and how to submit one, see [AI Assessments (AIA) guidance (Red Hat employees only · VPN required)](https://url.corp.redhat.com/ai-assessments).
